Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service free?

Yes, everything is free to use. Our goal is to spread spiritual knowledge and make the Gita more accessible.

How are the verses translated?

We use trusted Sanskrit-English translations from scholarly sources. AI helps present them in a reader-friendly format.

How are the Shlokas found?

When you enter a keyword or topic, our AI searches a structured dataset of all 700 Bhagavad Gita shlokas and matches the most relevant one based on context and semantics.

What is the source of the shlokas?

The shlokas are from the complete Bhagavad Gita, validated against public domain translations and verified Sanskrit texts.

Can I search by chapter and verse?

Yes! You can directly enter chapter and verse numbers (e.g., 2.47) to access specific teachings from the Gita.
