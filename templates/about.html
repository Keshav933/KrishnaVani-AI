ABOUT KRISHNAVANI

Connecting You With the Wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita

KrishnaVani is a digital platform created to make the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita easier to explore, search, understand, and learn.

ॐ

Our Purpose

The Bhagavad Gita contains timeless wisdom about life, duty, knowledge, action, devotion, and self-realization. KrishnaVani provides a simple digital interface where users can explore these teachings without needing to remember the complete verse.

श्लोक

What You Can Explore

KrishnaVani provides multiple ways to interact with the Bhagavad Gita. You can search for shlokas using keywords, find a specific chapter and verse, and explore the meaning of individual verses.

01

Shloka Search

Search for verses using words or phrases from the Bhagavad Gita.

02

Verse Navigator

Directly access a verse using its chapter and verse number.

03

Translation

Explore translations and meanings to better understand the teachings.

AI

Technology Behind KrishnaVani

The project combines a Flask-based web application with natural language and machine-learning components. A trained neural network model is included in the project for experimentation with Bhagavad Gita text and language generation.
